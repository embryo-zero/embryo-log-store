# Embryo Log Store

Embryo Log Store is Embryo-Zero's fork of [loki](https://github.com/grafana/loki) (9e1b3d03, commit 9e1b3d03). It does keeps and searches every machine's logs.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE, LICENSING.md) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
